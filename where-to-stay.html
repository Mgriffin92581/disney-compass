Booking tips

  1. Compare the total trip cost, not the nightly rate. Parking, resort fees, and transport can change the math.
  2. Choose a refundable rate when you can, then recheck prices closer to your dates.
  3. Confirm what is included with your stay on the official Disney site, since perks change.
  4. Read recent guest reviews for the exact property you are booking.

Quick answers

Is staying on Disney property worth it?

It is worth it if convenience matters most to you. Staying nearby is often cheaper and gives you more room, so compare both for your dates.

How far in advance should I book?

Popular dates fill up. Booking a refundable rate early, then rechecking prices, is a common approach.

Do I need a car?

Not always. Disney resorts offer transport, while off-property stays may need a car or shuttle. Check each property's options.
