Pack for the Florida weather

Small items make long park days much easier.

  • Broken-in walking shoes, since you can cover many miles a day
  • Portable phone charger for the app, photos, and mobile tickets
  • Lightweight rain ponchos for afternoon storms
  • Clip-on mini fan for the summer queues
  • Small crossbody or park bag that is easy to carry on rides
  • Insulated water bottle to refill through the day

Quick answers

How many days do I need?

It depends on how many parks you want and how fast you like to go. Many families plan around one day per park plus a rest day. Check current park offerings before you decide.

Is a park hopper worth it?

It helps if you want to visit more than one park in a day. If you prefer a relaxed pace, one park per day is often enough.

Are prices and policies on this site current?

Prices, ticket types, and rules change often. Always confirm the latest details on the official Disney site before booking.
